SemiconductorsUpdated 13 Oct 2026

Chip sales hit $159.7B in August, up 144% year over year.

Semiconductor manufacturers are taking steps to accommodate the increased demand driven by this year's AI infrastructure boom, prompting a wave of fab expansions and new construction projects.

The resulting surge in equipment orders has placed significant pressure on supply chains, lengthening delivery schedules at major equipment suppliers. A shipment that would have been delivered in six months now takes up to a year to arrive, significantly slowing manufacturers' efforts to expand production lines and increase capacity.

The objective of keeping capacity ahead of demand is becoming increasingly difficult for advanced node manufacturers due to these equipment bottlenecks. Foundries and IDMs are struggling to perfect manufacturing allocation, as they work to balance supporting the most advanced products with serving clients working in less active market segments.

Between delayed production expansions and tightening supply as manufacturers adjust allocation, supply relief will likely remain out of reach for the foreseeable future.
